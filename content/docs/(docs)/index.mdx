Welcome to the IT Chapter's system documentation!

Here, you can find documentation for the various systems built for and by the IT Chapter, and other information about the chapter.
The purpose is to provide a comprehensive overview of the IT Chapter's systems and
their respective functionalities. It also reduces the dependency on old students, ensuring the longevity of
the systems and reducing duplicate work.

The docs are open to the entire chapter to contribute.

If you are unsure about anything, do not hesitate to reach out to the person listed in the footer or the relevant committee.

## Is something missing?

Not everything is documented, so if something is missing, feel free to [open an issue](https://github.com/itsektionen/docs/issues).

If you have information that is not present in the docs, do not hesitate to [write some documentation for it](./writing-documentation/index.mdx)
